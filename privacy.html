Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

🔒 Short version: We collect the minimum data needed to run PLAYR. We don't sell your data. Ever.

01 Information We Collect

When you use PLAYR, we may collect:

  • Account info: Email, display name, profile photo
  • Game submissions: Content, links, and metadata you submit
  • Usage data: Pages viewed, games played, preferences
  • Device info: Browser type, screen size, OS (anonymized)
  • Cookies: For theme preferences and login sessions

02 How We Use Your Information

We use collected data to:

  • Provide and improve the PLAYR platform
  • Review and publish game submissions
  • Send notifications you've opted into
  • Prevent abuse and enforce our Terms
  • Analyze usage to improve user experience

03 Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Firebase (Google): For authentication, database, and hosting
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or valid legal process
  • Safety: To prevent harm, fraud, or abuse

04 Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser storage to:

  • Remember your theme preference (dark/light)
  • Keep you logged in across sessions
  • Cache site settings for faster loading

You can clear this data anytime in your browser settings.

05 Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encryption and Firebase's secure infrastructure. However, no online service is 100% secure. Please use a strong, unique password.

06 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and data
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of non-essential notifications

To delete your account, use the Settings page or contact us.

07 Children's Privacy

PLAYR is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us immediately.

08 Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days, except where legally required to keep it longer.

09 Third-Party Services

PLAYR uses Google Firebase for authentication and storage. Please review Firebase's Privacy Policy for details.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We'll notify you of significant changes via email or an in-app announcement.

11 Contact Us

Questions about privacy? Email us at privacy@playr.app or visit our Contact page.
